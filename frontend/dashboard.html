💡 Routine Silage Best Practice: The Squeeze Test

Grab a handful of chopped forage and squeeze firmly for 20-30 seconds. If juice runs freely between fingers, moisture exceeds 75% (risk of seepage and clostridia). If the ball expands slowly without juice, moisture is optimal (~65-70%). If the ball springs open immediately, crop is too dry (<60%, risk of mold).

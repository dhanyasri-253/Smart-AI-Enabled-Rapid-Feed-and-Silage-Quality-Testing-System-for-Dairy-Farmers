What the FQI Number Represents

The Fermentation Quality Index (FQI) is a continuous score formulated by agricultural researchers (University of Padova) to evaluate the completeness of anaerobic preservation. It is mathematically derived from volatile fatty acids (lactic, acetic, propionic, butyric), pH, and ammonia nitrogen (NH₃-N / Total N). Higher numerical values reflect predominantly lactic acid preservation with minimal protein breakdown.

Model Limitations & Research Disclaimer

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE: This Random Forest regression model (n_estimators=500, max_depth=10) was trained on 1,486 research trial observations from the University of Padova. Under rigorous 5-fold Leave-One-Trial-Out cross-validation (GroupKFold on ref.trial), its average out-of-trial R² remained negative (-0.580 ± 1.068, MAE = 9.51), reflecting biological domain shifts between research campaigns. The model has NOT been calibrated on Indian fodder cultivars or tropical agro-climatic zones. In compliance with scientific integrity, no artificial accuracy percentages or Good/Moderate/Poor tiers were invented. This prediction does NOT prove silage is safe for animal feeding.
